## 1. Dignity Funerals (dignityfunerals.co.uk)

- **Navigation:** four top-level items only: *Funerals · Funeral Plans · Legal Services · Guidance*. Footer: *Crematoria and cemeteries, Ashes finder, Pay for a funeral, Complaints, Accessibility*.
- **Tone:** reassuring and plain. *"If someone has died, you don't have to figure it all out alone… take things one step at a time."* The guide opens: *"First, take a breath and remember that there are people here to support you."*
- **Urgent path:** a home-page section, "What to do when someone dies", links to a 5-minute guide with numbered steps (Immediately after → Registering → Arranging → Estate) and an "On this page" index. The 24-hour message sits inside the guide (*"available 24 hours a day, every day of the year"*). The national 0800 number appears only in the footer; the main call to action is a postcode **"Find a funeral director"** search.
- **Pricing:** no prices on the home page.
- **Forms:** a single-field town/postcode branch finder, repeated three times.
- **Trust signals:** *"over 200 years"*, *"over 500 branches"*, a Trustpilot widget, and FCA-regulation text in the footer.
- **Imagery:** real photography with detailed alt text: funeral directors in top hats beside a hearse, held hands, flowers on a coffin, two women talking in a garden.
- **Good:** very short top navigation; a calm, stepwise guide with a reading time and anchor index. **Weaker:** the urgent path depends on a postcode search rather than a phone number; there is no tap-to-call above the fold.

## 2. Pure Cremation (purecremation.co.uk)

- **Navigation:** *Funeral plans* (Our funeral plan, What happens, Savings calculator, Funeral plan guide) · *Arrange a funeral* (Arrange a funeral today, What to do guide, What is direct cremation, Our locations) · *Guides, videos & more* · *About us* (Code of Practice, Meet the team, Customer reviews) · *Contact*.
- **Tone:** two registers. The arrange page is warm: *"We're here 24/7 if you need help arranging a funeral… Just one call to us"*. The plan pages are sales-led: an *"OFFER ENDS IN"* countdown banner and *"HURRY"*.
- **Urgent path:** a clear H1, "We're here 24/7", with *"Tell us someone has died"* and a free 0800 number repeated as an image button. *"A Pure Care Companion means you talk to the same person throughout."* A numbered "How does our funeral service work?" sequence.
- **Pricing:** strongly price-led. A *"Pure Price Promise"* (no hidden extras), a savings calculator, a *"Cost of Funerals Index"*, and monthly instalments.
- **Forms:** "Get your quick quote" calls to action; savings calculator.
- **Trust signals:** *"Best Funeral Plan Provider 2024/25 and 2025/26"*, Trustpilot and Reviews.io logos, *"40,000 five star reviews"*, money *"held in a Trust… reinsured"*, a Code of Practice page.
- **Good:** an unmistakable 24/7 call to action, an explanation of what happens step by step, a calculator. **Avoid:** countdown or urgency tactics next to bereavement content, and phone numbers rendered as images (not accessible, can't be tapped or copied).

## 3. Funeral Guide (funeralguide.co.uk)

- **Navigation:** *Find a Funeral Director · Obituaries · Help & Advice · Log in*, plus a trade link, "List your funeral home".
- **Tone:** neutral and informational. The H1 is *"Find the right funeral director"*.
- **Pricing:** *"£ Upfront pricing so you know the cost"*, *"Compare services before you make a call"*, and FAQ answers with average costs (e.g. *"average cost of cremation in the UK is £854"*).
- **Forms:** location search, both in the header and in the hero.
- **Trust signals:** *"★★★★★ 133,299 reviews"*, *"Verified reviews from bereaved families"*, a live feed of dated, named reviews of individual funeral directors, and "As featured in" press logos.
- **Content:** an advice hub (When someone dies, Arranging a funeral, Managing your estate, Government services, Bereavement support) and long location-page lists for SEO.
- **Good:** fresh, specific reviews that name staff (*"Incredibly helpful, especially Richard"*); FAQ content that answers cost questions directly; location pages. **Lesson for us:** encourage Google reviews and quote them; build genuine town pages.

## 4. CPJ Field (cpjfield.co.uk), independent since 1690

- **Navigation:** *Find a Funeral Home* (by county, then town and trading name) · *What To Do When Someone Dies, Registering a Death, Choosing a Funeral Home, FAQs* · *Planning a Funeral* (Our Service, Types of funeral, **Funeral Costs**, Planning the Service, Prepaid Plans, Memorials) · *Guides & Advice* (After the Funeral, Bereavement Support, Glossary) · *Current Funerals* · *About*.
- **Tone:** family and personal. *"Looking after families like yours, for generations… here day or night."* On costs: *"Tell us your budget at the start, and we'll work within it."*
- **Urgent path:** the call-back form's first question is *"How can we help?"*: *Someone has died / I need to arrange a funeral · I'm planning a funeral in advance · Headstone or memorial · Something else / I'm not sure*. Its helper text reads *"This makes sure the right person calls you."* and *"Rather talk than type? Call us now… We answer day and night."* It is a multi-step form offering phone, in-person or video.
- **Pricing:** a model for transparency. Costs are split into **funeral director costs / third-party costs (disbursements) / optional extras**, with a list of what the fees include and *Traditional Funeral from £4,135* and *Unattended Funeral from £2,734*. *"We'll always provide you a written estimate before anything is booked, and we'll explain every line of it."*
- **Trust signals:** *"since 1690"*, logos for NAFD, SAIF, Order of the Golden Rule, the European Federation of Funeral Services and FITA (each with descriptive alt text), and a Google Review link.
- **Good:** honest cost structure and budget language; a call-back form that routes by situation; "Current Funerals" (notices) in the main navigation. **Weaker:** the very long location mega-menu is repeated, which adds page weight on mobile.

## What we take into the Irish design

| Pattern | Source | How we use it |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number tappable on every screen, 24/7 stated plainly | Pure, CPJ Field | Top bar, sticky mobile call bar, hero buttons. Real text, never an image. |
| "Someone has died" page with numbered steps by place of death | Dignity, Pure | `/someone-has-died/`: home / hospital / sudden death (Gardaí and coroner, Irish process). |
| Our fees, third-party costs and optional extras shown separately | CPJ Field | `/prices/`: itemised tables plus an Irish third-party price guide. |
| Calculator | Pure | `/pre-planning/#calculator`, without sales pressure and with ranges, not a single number. |
| Situation-first routing question | CPJ Field | Call-back "What is it about?" field routes to on-call or office. |
| Fresh, named reviews | Funeral Guide | Google reviews feed on the home page (placeholders now). |
| Short top navigation | Dignity | 8 items, "Someone has died" first and highlighted. |
| Notices in the main navigation | CPJ Field | "Funeral notices", linked to RIP.ie. |
| **Avoid:** countdown offers, "HURRY", phone numbers as images | Pure (plan pages) | No urgency marketing anywhere on the site. |
