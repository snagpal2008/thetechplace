# 06 · Images: shot list and rules

## Rules

1. **Use only** (a) photographs the funeral home owns or commissions, with a written release from anyone pictured, or (b) original or licence-free images with a clear licence recorded in `site/IMAGE_CREDITS.md`.
2. **Never** copy photographs from other funeral websites, including the UK sites in our research.
3. **No stock images of grieving faces, crying, or coffins being lowered.** Use calm landscapes, hands and care, flowers, light, and the real premises and team.
4. Every image: **WebP**, three widths (480 / 960 / 1600 px), explicit `width` and `height`, `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"` below the fold, and `fetchpriority="high"` with a preload for the hero only.
5. **Alt text:** describe what matters in one plain sentence. Decorative hero backgrounds use `alt=""` because the H1 carries the meaning.
6. Under 200 KB per 1600 px image. To convert: `cwebp -q 78 in.jpg -o out-1600.webp`, or run `tools/generate-images.py` as a pattern.

## Shot list for the photographer

| Page | Shot | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Home: hero** | Wide landscape near the town at dawn or golden hour: hills, river or bog; soft light | Leave calm space on the left for text. Landscape 16:9. |
| Home: family | **Premises exterior**: front door, signage, flowers in the window | Daytime, overcast for soft light. Also one at dusk with the windows lit. |
| Home / About | **The family**: the generations together outside the funeral home | Natural and gently smiling. Not posed in top hats. |
| About: team | **Individual portraits** of each staff member, 1:1, plain background | Same light and crop for all. Replace the initials avatars. |
| About: story | Archive photo of the founder or the original premises | Scan at 600 dpi. Credit "Family archive". |
| Someone has died | **Hands and care**: one hand resting gently on another; a cup of tea on a table in the arrangement room | Never show a face in distress. |
| Services: burial | Parish church exterior; a local cemetery gate in soft light | Obtain parish permission. |
| Services: cremation | Crematorium grounds and a garden of remembrance | Ask the crematorium's permission. |
| Services: direct cremation | A simple urn on a linen cloth with a single flower | Still life. |
| Services: aftercare | A headstone mason's workshop, hands on stone | |
| Prices | Arrangement room: table, chairs, brochure, natural light | It shows "a calm place to talk". |
| Funeral notices | A lit candle; a book of condolence with a pen | Still life. |
| Pre-planning | Two people at a kitchen table with a notebook (hands and backs only) | Warm, ordinary. |
| Support | Woodland walk, a bench, the sea | Landscapes from the local area. |
| Contact | Front door with step-free access visible; car park | It shows accessibility. |
| Flowers (various) | White lilies, roses, wildflower spray on a coffin (coffin edge only) | Work with a local florist and credit them. |

**Release forms:** a model release from every identifiable person; property releases for churches and crematoria; written permission from families before any funeral is photographed. The default is never to photograph funerals.
