## Design reference

Use **https://bennettpros.com/listings/** (and its home page) as the **style and quality benchmark only**: premium personal-brand feel, large photographic hero, clean card grids, sticky header with a clear call to action, generous whitespace, confident typography. Do **not** copy its text, images, logos or code, and do **not** reproduce its MLS® listing search.

Respect **Royal LePage brand standards**: use the official Royal LePage logo and red only as permitted, keep Harpreet's personal brand secondary-but-distinct, and do not alter the Royal LePage mark. Flag anything that needs brokerage or Royal LePage brand approval before launch.

## Hard constraints

1. **No MLS® / IDX / DDF® integration.** No listing feeds, listing search, map search or listing widgets. Link out to Harpreet's realtor.ca and Royal LePage profiles for current listings instead.
2. **Individual site for Harpreet Singh only.** No other agents' pages. The brokerage is shown where the rules require it (below).
3. **Never fabricate anything.** Every review, award, statistic, sale and testimonial must come from the Inputs. Missing input → visible `TODO` placeholder, listed in the final checklist.
4. **Reviews are verbatim**, with first name + last initial, date and source, linked to the original where possible.
5. **Ontario advertising rules (TRESA / RECO):**
   - Brokerage name **exactly as registered**, clearly and prominently on every page (header or footer) and on every ad-like element.
   - Harpreet's name exactly as registered and the title "Salesperson"/"Sales Representative"; never "agent", "broker" or "team" titles he doesn't hold.
   - No misleading claims (e.g. "#1" or "top" without a verifiable source, year and issuer shown alongside).
   - Footer line: "Not intended to solicit buyers or sellers currently under contract with a brokerage."
   - Royal LePage franchise line if required by the brokerage: "Independently owned and operated."
6. **CREA trademarks:** use REALTOR® and MLS® correctly (with ®) and include CREA's standard trademark notice in the footer.
7. **Mortgage content:** Harpreet is a real-estate salesperson. Mortgage pages are **general education only** and must say so. Do not quote rates or give mortgage advice in his name unless an FSRA licence is listed in Inputs. Calculators carry an "estimates only" disclaimer. If a mortgage partner is listed, name them with their FSRA licence number and disclose the referral relationship.
8. **Privacy:** PIPEDA-compliant privacy policy, consent checkbox on every form, CASL-compliant opt-in (unchecked by default) for any newsletter or email marketing.

## Social integration (YouTube + Facebook)

- **YouTube:** lite embed (thumbnail + play button; loads the `youtube-nocookie.com` iframe only on click). Featured video on Home, full gallery on Videos. `VideoObject` schema per video.
- **Facebook:** official Page plugin and post/video embeds on Reviews and About, **loaded only after cookie consent**. Facebook/YouTube/Instagram icons in header and footer.
- Facebook and Google reviews also rendered as **static HTML** so they're indexed, load instantly and survive if an embed breaks.
- Open Graph / Twitter card tags on every page so links look good when shared on Facebook and WhatsApp.

## Best-in-class quality bar

- **Design:** premium, warm and trustworthy, harmonised with Royal LePage red without overusing it (e.g. charcoal/navy base, Royal LePage red as accent, off-white backgrounds). Modern serif headings + clean sans body. Real photos of Harpreet and Ottawa neighbourhoods over generic stock. Subtle motion only, respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Mobile-first:** sticky bottom bar on phones with Call / WhatsApp / Book; tap targets ≥ 44px; no horizontal scroll.
- **Performance:** Lighthouse ≥ 95 in all four categories on mobile; LCP < 2.0s; CLS < 0.05. Responsive WebP/AVIF images with width/height; lazy-load below the fold; third-party scripts deferred until interaction/consent; no heavy page builders.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.2 AA and AODA-aligned — contrast, keyboard navigation, focus states, labelled forms with clear errors, alt text, captions where available.
- **SEO / local SEO:** unique titles and meta descriptions; `RealEstateAgent` + `Person` + `Review`/`AggregateRating` (first-party reviews only) + `FAQPage` schema; neighbourhood pages targeting "Kanata real estate agent", "Ottawa first-time home buyer", etc.; XML sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs; optional Punjabi/Hindi pages with `hreflang`.
- **Conversion:** a CTA visible on every screen; short forms; instant confirmation + email alert to Harpreet (optional SMS/WhatsApp); spam protection (honeypot + Cloudflare Turnstile); consent-aware analytics events on calls, WhatsApp taps, bookings and form submissions.
- **Security & privacy:** HTTPS, security headers, minimal data collection, clear retention statement.
- **Maintainability:** contact details, brokerage info, social links, reviews, awards, neighbourhoods and past sales in **one data file**, so they can be updated without touching layout code.
