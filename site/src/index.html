How we will be with you

No rush. No pressure. Just the help you need.

Grief is not something to be managed or hurried. When you come to us, we will listen first, explain everything plainly, and let you decide at your own pace.

  • We will always use your loved one's name.
  • We will tell you what things cost before you decide, in writing.
  • We will follow your family's customs and faith, or none.
  • We will still be here after the funeral: for the month's mind, the anniversary, and the days in between.
