Planning ahead

Make your wishes known

Planning your own funeral can be a kindness to the people you love. It means fewer decisions for them at a hard time, and the reassurance that it will be done your way.

How planning ahead works

1. Record your wishes

Burial or cremation, music, readings, where you'd like to repose. Note as much or as little as you like.

2. Know the cost

Use the calculator below for a guide price at today's rates. Your plan includes a written estimate.

3. Pay later or now

Keep your wishes on file free of charge. Or pay in advance into a protected account to fix our fees at today's prices.
