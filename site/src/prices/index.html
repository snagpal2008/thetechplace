How a funeral is priced

1. Our fees

Our professional service, care of your loved one, the coffin, the hearse and our staff. These are listed below.

2. Third-party costs

Fees we pay to others on your behalf, such as the cemetery, crematorium, church and death notices. We pass these on at cost, with no mark-up.

3. Your choices

Flowers, limousines, printed booklets, music, live-streaming and catering. Every one of them is optional.

Help with funeral costs

If you are worried about paying for a funeral, please tell us early. We will work within your budget. You may be able to get help through the Department of Social Protection's Exceptional Needs Payment, from the person's estate or bank, or through a pension or insurance policy.
