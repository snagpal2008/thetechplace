A funeral that feels like them

Every family is different. We will explain your choices simply, without pressure, and follow your wishes and traditions.

Traditional funeral and burial

The Irish funeral as most families know it: reposing at home or in our funeral home, the removal to the church, funeral Mass or service, and burial.

  • Wake at home or in our chapel of rest
  • Removal, hearse and limousine
  • Liaison with your priest, minister or celebrant and the cemetery

Cremation funeral

A full funeral service in a church, at the crematorium or in our chapel, followed by cremation. We will return the ashes to you in an urn of your choice.

  • Ceremony in a church, at the crematorium or in our chapel
  • Help choosing music, readings and a celebrant
  • Ashes returned personally, or interred or scattered
Prices for cremation

Direct cremation

A simple, dignified cremation with no funeral service beforehand. Families often choose it to keep costs down, or to hold their own memorial later in a place that means something to them.

  • Collection, care and a simple coffin
  • All paperwork and the cremation fee
  • Ashes returned to you
