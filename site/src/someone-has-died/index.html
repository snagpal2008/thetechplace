What to do first

What happens next depends on where the person died. You can always call us first and we will guide you.

If they died at home

  1. Call their GP (or the out-of-hours doctor service). A doctor needs to confirm the death. If the death was expected, this can wait until morning.
  2. Call us on {{phone247}}. Once the doctor has visited, we will come to the house and bring your loved one into our care, or help you to wake them at home if you wish.
  3. If the death was sudden or unexpected, call 999 or 112. The Gardaí and the coroner may need to be involved. This is routine, and we will explain each step.

If they died in hospital, a hospice or a nursing home

  1. The staff will confirm the death and look after the medical certificate.
  2. Call us when you are ready. We will speak to the hospital or home directly and arrange to bring your loved one to our funeral home.

In the next few days

  • Meet us (at our funeral home, your home, or by video) to arrange the funeral. We will give you a written, itemised estimate.
  • We will help you write the death notice for RIP.ie and local papers.
  • Register the death with the Civil Registration Service. A close relative usually does this, within 3 months and often after the funeral. See who registers a death and what to bring.
  • Find out about financial help, such as the Exceptional Needs Payment, at Citizens Information.

Wakes at home, removals, Mass and burial or cremation: whatever your family's customs or faith, we will follow your lead.
