Bereavement support

You don't have to grieve alone

Grief has no timetable. Here are people who can help, whenever you need them, whether that is next week or next year.

If you need to talk now

  • Samaritans: free, 24 hours, 116 123
  • Pieta (suicide and self-harm, including bereavement by suicide): 1800 247 247 or text HELP to 51444
  • In an emergency, call 112 or 999

Grief and bereavement

  • Irish Hospice Foundation: bereavement information, and the Bereavement Support Line
  • Barnardos: support for bereaved children and young people
  • HSE: bereavement and mental-health services

Practical matters

  • Citizens Information: registering a death, wills, estates and entitlements
  • Department of Social Protection: pensions, Exceptional Needs Payment and household benefits
  • Courts Service: probate

From us

  • Grief-support letters over the first year: sign up
  • Our annual remembrance service each November
  • Help with month's minds, anniversary Masses and headstones

Please check these links and numbers before launch. Services change their details from time to time.
